# The AATU's statement in support of Desirae Simmons

The Ann Arbor Tenants Union stands in full support of Desirae Simmons
and opposes the recall campaign by Ypsilanti Forward.

Dez is a strong and consistent supporter of community activism,
tenants’ rights, and affordable housing. Dez ran on a campaign of
affordable housing and kept her word when she entered office — to the
dismay of those opposed to a sustainable economy for all.

Analyses from What’s Left Ypsi have revealed that 89% of the recall
campaign donors are landlords and 79% of recall campaign dollars came
from landlords; the rest are other lobbyists including Republican
disinformation campaign engineers like Public Square Productions. We
stand against Ypsi elites, for Section 8 Housing, and for the
community voices Dez has always championed.
